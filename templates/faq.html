Frequently Asked Questions

What factors influence customer churn?

  • Credit Score: Lower scores may increase churn risk.
  • Age & Tenure: Younger customers or those with shorter tenures are more likely to leave.
  • Balance & Salary: High balances with low salaries can signal higher churn risk.
  • Number of Products: Customers using more products tend to be more loyal.
  • Activity Status: Active members are less likely to churn.

How accurate is the prediction model?

Our model is trained on a robust dataset using advanced machine learning techniques, ensuring high accuracy. Results depend on the quality of input data provided.

Who can benefit from Churn Predictor?

Businesses across industries can use Churn Predictor to gain insights into customer behavior, optimize retention strategies, and improve overall performance.
